# Round 6 — Behavioral (STAR)
**Interview:** AI / Dev-Tools Startup
**Duration:** 45 minutes

---

## Question 1 — Conflict / Feedback You Disagreed With
> "Tell me about feedback you received on CodeSync AI's architecture — from a mentor, peer, or code reviewer — that you initially disagreed with. What was the feedback, why did you push back, and what happened?"

---

## Question 2 — Failure / Security or Stability Issue
> "Was there a security or stability issue you discovered late in your Docker sandbox implementation — something you missed on the first pass? Walk me through what it was and how you found it."

*If no Docker security issue:* "Tell me about a stability bug in CodeSync AI's real-time sync that took longer to fix than expected."

---

## Question 3 — Pressure / Flipkart GRiD
> "Flipkart GRiD 8.0 — walk me through the time crunch. How did you decide what to build vs what to cut? What was left on the table?"

---

## Question 4 — Ambiguity / No Right Answer
> "How did you decide which 3 programming languages to support in CodeSync AI's sandbox, and which sandboxing tradeoffs to accept? Was there a spec, or did you make those calls yourself?"

---

## Question 5 — Initiative / Time Management
> "You built 3 substantial personal projects, completed a production internship, maintained a CGPA of 8.11, and solved 1,500+ CP problems. Be honest with me — how did you actually allocate your time? What did you sacrifice?"

---

## Scoring Rubric
| Dimension | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| Specificity | Concrete events, actual code/decisions referenced | "I faced challenges and overcame them" |
| Self-awareness | Knows what they would do differently | Retrospectively perfect in every story |
| Coachability | Feedback changed their approach | "I was right and proved it" |
| Communication | Clear structure, no rambling, finishes in <3 min per answer | Goes off-topic, loses the point |
