# Round 7 — Hiring Manager / Bar Raiser
**Interview:** EdTech / Learning Platform Company
**Duration:** 45-60 minutes

---

## Resume Defense
"'Mentored 400+ students' — over what period, how do you know it was 400 and not an estimate, and what's your actual evidence mentoring worked, not just that it happened?"

## Judgment Scenarios
1. A mentor on the platform gives biased/incomplete feedback, but engagement metrics for their class are great. What do you do?
2. Auto-grading gave a student a 0 due to a grading-script bug the night before a scholarship deadline. Immediate response, to the student and to the system?

## Close
EdTech interest tied to your mentoring background, expectations.
