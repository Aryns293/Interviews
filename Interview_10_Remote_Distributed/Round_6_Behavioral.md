# Round 6 — Behavioral (STAR)
**Interview:** Remote-First / Fully Distributed Team Company

---

## Q1 — Conflict / Remote Resolution
> "A disagreement during your remote Rolewize internship — how do you resolve conflict with someone you've never met face-to-face?"

*Expected:* Assume positive intent. In text, a short response can sound angry when it's just rushed. Default to hopping on a quick synchronous call if a Slack thread goes past 5 replies without resolution. 

---

## Q2 — Failure / Async Miscommunication
> "Tell me about something that went wrong specifically because of async/remote miscommunication, not because of a coding mistake."

*Expected:* An honest story about building the wrong thing because you made an assumption about a spec, and didn't want to wait 12 hours for the product manager in the US to wake up and confirm it. The lesson: it's better to be blocked for 12 hours and work on a side task than to build the wrong feature for 2 days.

---

## Q3 — Pressure / Self-Managing Deadlines
> "Self-managing a deadline during a remote internship with no one checking in on you in real time — how'd you know you were on track?"

*Expected:* Personal tracking systems. Breaking down a 2-week feature into daily deliverables. If you couldn't hit the daily deliverable, raising a flag *that day*, not waiting for the sprint review.

---

## Q4 — Ambiguity / Remote Onboarding
> "Remote onboarding with unclear expectations — how'd you figure out what actually mattered without being able to just walk over and ask?"

*Expected:* Proactive communication. Setting up 15-minute intro calls with key stakeholders. Asking specific questions like "What does success look like for me in week 2?" rather than "What should I do?"

---

## Q5 — Initiative / Proactive Documentation
> "Give me an example of proactively documenting a decision so an async teammate could follow your reasoning without needing to ask you directly."

*Expected:* Mention Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) or writing detailed Pull Request descriptions. "In QueueFlow, I documented exactly why I used AOF instead of RDB for Redis persistence in the PR description, so the reviewer didn't have to ask."
