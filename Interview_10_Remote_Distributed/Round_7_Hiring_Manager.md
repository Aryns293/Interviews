## Resume Defense — Remote Trust

> "Rolewize — 'Remote,' two months, shipping production webhook infrastructure with real security requirements. How did you build enough trust for that responsibility with a team that had never worked with you before, that fast?"

*What I'm testing:* Remote work requires high trust because observability of your work is low.
*Strong answer:* "I built trust by being predictable. I told them what I was going to do in the morning, I did it, and I posted a short summary at the end of the day. When I got stuck, I didn't hide it, I escalated it clearly with the steps I had already tried. After a week of that consistency, they trusted me to take on the webhook delivery system."

## Judgment Scenario — The Solo Bad Call

> "You made a technical call solo — genuinely alone, because nobody in your timezone was online. It turned out to be the wrong call. By the time your team saw it, it had already shipped. What do you do?"

*Expected:*
1. **Own it completely.** Don't say "I had to do it because no one was online." Say "I made a judgment call to unblock myself, and I got it wrong."
2. **Mitigate immediately.** Revert the commit or toggle the feature flag if it's breaking production.
3. **Write the post-mortem.** Document *why* you thought it was the right call at the time. This helps the team adjust docs so the next person in your timezone doesn't make the same mistake.
4. **Accept feedback.** Thank the reviewer for catching it.
