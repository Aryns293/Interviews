# Round 6 — Behavioral (STAR)
**Interview:** General Product-Based Company
**Duration:** 45 minutes

> **Rule for every answer in this round:** You must anchor it to a specific real experience — Rolewize, one of your 3 projects, SIH, or GRiD. Generic answers will get pushed back immediately.

---

## Q1 — Conflict
> "Tell me about a time you disagreed with someone — a teammate, a senior, a mentor — on a technical or product decision. Be specific about what you disagreed on and why."

*After your answer:* "What would you do differently if the same situation came up again?"

---

## Q2 — Failure
> "Tell me about a project, feature, or submission that didn't go the way you planned. What happened and what did you learn?"

*Push-back I'll use:* "That sounds like it turned out okay in the end. Tell me about something that actually failed — where the outcome was bad and stayed bad."

---

## Q3 — Pressure
> "Describe a time you were working under a tight deadline. Walk me through your day-to-day prioritization decisions, not just the outcome."

---

## Q4 — Ambiguity
> "Tell me about a decision you made on one of your projects where there was no obvious right answer. What was it, what did you decide, and do you stand by it?"

*Examples that work well:* MongoDB vs Postgres choice, Redis vs Kafka for QueueFlow, which Git commands to implement first in gitlight, which languages to sandbox in CodeSync AI.

---

## Q5 — Initiative
> "Tell me about a time you did something nobody asked you to do — technical or otherwise. Why did you do it, and what was the impact?"

---

## Scoring Rubric
| Dimension | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| Specificity | Named project, named technology, named outcome | "I worked on a team project and faced a challenge" |
| STAR structure | Situation → Task → Action → Result, clearly | Long rambling story with no clear resolution |
| Self-awareness | Owns the failure, knows what to change | Retrospectively perfect, blame on others |
| Conciseness | Each answer is 2–3 minutes | 7-minute monologues |
