# Round 7 — Hiring Manager / Bar Raiser
**Interview:** General Product-Based Company

---

## Resume Defense (Random Pick)

**I'll pick one of these at random:**

**Option A — CGPA / Academic Performance**
> "You have an 8.11 CGPA. Where did marks slip — which subjects, and why?"

**Option B — GRiD 8.0 Selection**
> "You were selected for Flipkart GRiD. What was the problem statement, what did you build specifically, and what didn't make it in due to time?"

**Option C — Mentoring 400+ Students in C++**
> "You mentored 400+ students. What was the format — individual sessions, workshops, online? What's the one concept where you saw the most students struggle, and what did you do differently to explain it?"

**Option D — Any Project Bullet**
> I pick one specific number from any project bullet and ask you to justify it:
> - "What's the 24-hour TTL based on? Why not 12 hours or 48 hours?"
> - "3-attempt retry — is that empirically derived or just a common default?"
> - "You say 'zero data loss' — is that literally true?"

---

## Judgment Scenario

> "You're asked to ship a feature you believe is fundamentally broken — there's a bug you've found that affects one-in-ten users, but your manager says the deadline is non-negotiable. What do you do?"

**Strong answer:** Quantify and document the risk → propose a limited rollout (10% of users, monitored) → put the bug in a P0 ticket before shipping → make the decision explicit, not silent.

## Close

**Q1:** "Why are you interested in this specific company and role?"

**Q2:** "What kind of team environment do you do your best work in?"

**Q3:** "What are your salary expectations?" *(Light touch — it's fine to say "I'm flexible and open to discussion based on the role.")*

**Q4:** "Any questions for me?"

*Strong questions:*
- "What does the first 90 days look like for a new hire on this team?"
- "What's the biggest technical challenge this team is working through right now?"
- "How does the team balance shipping new features vs addressing tech debt?"
