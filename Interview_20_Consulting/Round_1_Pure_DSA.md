## Problem 1 — 3Sum (Expense Report Flagging)
**Difficulty:** Medium

**The Problem:**
3Sum — loosely reframed as finding 3 line items in a client expense report summing to a flagged total, really just testing clean two-pointer technique.

---

## Problem 2 — Coin Change (Component Assembly)
**Difficulty:** Hard (Reframed)

**The Problem:**
Coin Change (min coins for an amount, DP) — reframed as minimum pre-built component modules needed to assemble a client's requested feature, modules reusable.

**Mid-solve twist:**
> "The client changes the requested scope mid-conversation, after you've already started your DP table."

*Tests whether you can adapt an in-progress solution without restarting from scratch, and whether you narrate the adjustment clearly — much more a communication test here than a pure algorithm one.*

**Scored on:**
- Whether you confirm the requirement change back to the interviewer before recoding.
- Brute force stated first.
- Edge case of an unreachable amount.
