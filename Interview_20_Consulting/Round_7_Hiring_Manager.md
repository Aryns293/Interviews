**Duration:** 45-60 minutes

## Resume Defense
Pick any one line and defend it 3 levels deep — but framed as "imagine I'm a client, not an engineer — plain language first, then technical."

## Judgment Scenarios
1. A client stakeholder is happy with a feature you know has a real bug that hasn't surfaced yet. Ship on time and stay quiet, or delay and explain a problem they don't see yet?
2. You're the only technical person in a client meeting and get asked something you genuinely don't know, live. What do you actually say?

## Close
Comfort with variety over deep specialization, why consulting/client-services specifically versus a product company, expectations.
