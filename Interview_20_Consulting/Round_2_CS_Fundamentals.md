## Breadth vs Depth
Deliberately broad-then-deep, testing range:
- Quick-fire OS (process vs thread, deadlock basics).
- DBMS (ACID with a break-it example; a live SQL join across a schema you're shown for the first time).
- CN (REST idempotency, HTTP status codes).
- *Deep Dive:* The interviewer picks either your HMAC-SHA256 work or your Docker sandboxing and pushes it 3-4 follow-ups deep, same rigor as any specialist loop, testing that breadth hasn't cost you real depth anywhere.
