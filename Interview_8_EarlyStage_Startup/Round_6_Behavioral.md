# Round 6 — Behavioral (STAR)
**Interview:** Early-Stage Seed Startup
**Note:** In a startup loop, "behavioral" questions often look like technical prioritization questions. They want to know how you operate when no one is telling you what to do.

---

## Q1 — Conflict / Hackathon Teammate
> "Tell me about a disagreement with a hackathon teammate (like Flipkart GRiD or SIH) on scope or tech choice, with a deadline bearing down."

*What I'm listening for:* Did you stubbornly argue for the "best" tech, or did you compromise for the sake of shipping? Startups need the latter.

---

## Q2 — Failure / Hackathon Time Crunch
> "Tell me about something that didn't work in time during a hackathon or a project deadline. What happened, and what would you have dropped earlier if you could do it again?"

*Expected:* Honest reflection on over-scoping. The right answer is usually "I tried to build the perfect backend instead of mocking the data to get the frontend demo working."

---

## Q3 — Pressure / The GRiD/SIH Triage
> "The actual GRiD or SIH time crunch — how did you triage tasks when you realized you wouldn't finish everything?"

*Expected:* Ruthless prioritization. What is the core flow? (e.g., if it's an AI tool, the AI generation must work; user profiles and settings can be hardcoded).

---

## Q4 — Ambiguity / Underspecified Problem Statement
> "An underspecified hackathon problem statement — how did you decide what to build first?"

*Expected:* "I didn't guess. I mapped out the fastest way to get something clickable so we could see if the idea even made sense, rather than building infrastructure for a feature we might pivot away from."
